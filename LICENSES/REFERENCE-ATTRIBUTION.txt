Alba MacKenna: Merchant character performance, released CC BY 4.0 in kyutai/tts-voices/alba-mackenna/merchant.wav at the revision above. VoiceMatrix describes this original performance as Wayfarer Merchant; it is not an official game-character likeness. Source card: https://huggingface.co/kyutai/tts-voices/blob/323332d33f997de8394f24a193e1a76df720e01a/README.md .

Processing changes: remove initial silence, select at most 10 seconds of reference speech, downmix/resample to mono PCM16 at 22050 Hz, extract a 256-dimensional OpenVoice V2 speaker-conditioning vector, and store the same float16 values used during model inference. VoiceMatrix uses shared Phonon synthesis followed by neural tone-color conversion for these profiles. No separate network is trained per voice. Human naturalness, character resemblance and controlled listening results are not asserted by this notice.

Model/code licensing is separate from recording licensing. OpenVoice V2 and the Core ML conversion use their included MIT terms; Phonon and FluidAudio use their included Apache-2.0 terms.
