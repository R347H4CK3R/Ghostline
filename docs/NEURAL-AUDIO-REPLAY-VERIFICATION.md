# v0.9.8: Native neural WAV replay and evidence invalidation

PocketTTS and Kokoro share a native replay control after actual generated WAV bytes have passed the audio inspector. Before every replay, the app reads the saved WAV and revalidates both its SHA-256 container digest and the inspector's PCM payload digest. The old player's delegate is detached, playback stops, and playback-completed, listening-acknowledged, timestamp and report-export state are cleared. New replay sessions carry fresh identifiers so late callbacks from stopped players cannot attest the current playback. If integrity or playback setup fails, the app rejects replay and discards prior receipts.

These are **source and CI-tested safeguards**, not proof of audible generation on a physical iPhone or Strata. With an authorized voice preset, provision weights beforehand, disconnect all networks, generate on the target iPhone, tap replay, verify the button removes earlier acknowledgement, let playback complete, confirm listening, and privately export both WAV and SHA-bound JSON. Interrupt playback, tamper with the WAV, or change the preset to confirm previous evidence cannot persist. Record model provenance, rights and listening identity separately; zero authentic actor/celebrity/character identities have been validated by this change. RVC .index retrieval remains unsupported. Never ship private identity packs in public artifacts.
